SHIPPING TERMS

Flexible Shipping Terms

EXW

Ex Works

Buyer collects the goods from our facility and manages transportation, export and import formalities, and other logistics.

FOB

Free On Board

Buyer arranges the main freight from the port of shipment. Seller handles export clearance and delivery up to loading on board.

CIF

Cost, Insurance & Freight

We arrange and cover ocean freight and insurance up to the destination port.

CFR

Cost and Freight

We cover ocean freight to the destination port; buyer arranges marine insurance.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Our Spice Exports

Clear answers to common questions asked by global importers, wholesalers, and food manufacturing buyers regarding quality, MOQs, certifications, and international shipping.

We specialize in four primary export spice categories directly sourced from India's key agricultural hubs:

  • Dry Red Chilli: Guntur Teja (S17), Sannam S4 / 334, Byadgi, Wonder Hot.
  • Turmeric: Erode Finger & Bulb Turmeric (high curcumin content).
  • Black Pepper: Malabar & Tellicherry Garbled Berries (MG1, MUG, TGEB, TGSEB).
  • Green Cardamom: Western Ghats Bold Capsules (AGEB, AGB, AGS grades).

Every export shipment is backed by complete documentation and regulatory compliance:

  • IEC (Import Export Code) DGFT License
  • Spices Board of India Registration Cum Membership (RCMC)
  • FSSAI Food Safety Approval
  • FIEO (Federation of Indian Export Organisations) Membership
  • Official Phytosanitary Certificate & Certificate of Origin
  • SGS / Geo-Chem Third-Party Quality Inspection Reports upon request

We support flexible trade volumes to suit importers of all sizes. Our standard MOQs are:

  • Full Container Loads (FCL): 1x20ft FCL (~14 to 18 MT depending on product bulk density).
  • Less than Container Load (LCL): Supported for high-value items like Green Cardamom & Pepper.
  • Incoterms Supported: EXW (Ex Works), FOB (Free On Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), and CFR (Cost and Freight).

All batches undergo rigorous cleaning, de-stoning, color sorting, metal detection, and NABL-accredited laboratory testing for pesticide residue levels, aflatoxins, ochratoxin A, and heavy metals. We ensure full compliance with EU Regulation (EC) 396/2005 and US FDA ASTA quality guidelines.
